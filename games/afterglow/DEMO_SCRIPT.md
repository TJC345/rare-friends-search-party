# Search Party demo script

Target length: 35–40 seconds. Record at 1080p with sound on and no browser bookmarks or personal wallet details visible.

| Time | Picture | Voiceover / caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Wallet connected; open **My Rare Friends** and select a Friend. | **One prompt. One Rare Friend.** |
| 4–8s | Friend artwork appears on the island; pause on generation, family and specialty. | “Your Rare Friend becomes a field agent.” |
| 8–15s | Choose a destination, route and preparation; dispatch. | “Send it searching through the crypto internet.” |
| 15–20s | Friend travelling; briefly show the away timer. | “Then leave. Your Friend keeps searching while you are away.” |
| 20–28s | Use a completed short expedition and open the field bag. | “Return to artifacts, Rugged Relics and simulated RF field funds.” |
| 28–34s | Open the Friend's journal and artifact collection. | “Every Friend builds its own collection.” |
| 34–40s | Open **Signal** and show Season Zero mystery progress. End on the title. | “Together, every Friend helps decode the Silent Signal.” |

## Recording notes

- Use a Friend whose canonical artwork loads clearly and whose journal already has two or three varied discoveries.
- Record one uninterrupted flow where possible. Cut the expedition wait rather than showing the full timer.
- Keep **Preview only · RF and discoveries are simulated** visible during at least one economy interaction.
- Do not lead with menus or the FAQ. Lead with the Friend.
- End card: **RARE FRIENDS: SEARCH PARTY — YOUR FRIEND KEEPS SEARCHING.**
